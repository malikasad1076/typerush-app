1Why practice typing?

Typing speed affects how much you get done. Higher WPM means less time on mechanics, more on thinking.

Average adult: 40 WPM. Professional: 65–90 WPM. Competitive: 120+ WPM.

2How to improve fast

  • Touch type — stop looking at the keyboard.
  • Accuracy first — speed follows accuracy.
  • Check AI Trainer after every test.
  • Review errors after every test.
  • Practice daily — 10–15 minutes a day.
